Where it fits

A fixture holds a component in exactly the same position every time, so every part is machined alike. Power clamping holds the part faster and more consistently than manual clamping, which shortens loading time and reduces variation between operators.

We make fixtures for use on general machines and as part of our own special purpose machines. Milling and fine boring fixtures use hydraulic clamping, and indexing fixtures can be auto or manual.

How a project runs

  1. Study. We review the component drawing, the operations and the machine the fixture will be used on.
  2. Concept. The locating and clamping scheme is agreed with you.
  3. Build. The fixture is manufactured at our Aligarh works.
  4. Try-out. Part location and clamping are checked on your component.
  5. Delivery. The fixture is delivered ready to use.

Frequently asked questions

Which parts do you make fixtures for?

Automotive, tractor and two-wheeler, appliance, fan, sewing machine and lock parts. Customers have included Escorts, Eicher, Mahindra & Mahindra, Hindustan Motors and Telco.

Do you make fixtures for CNC machining centres?

Yes. Our range includes fixtures for CNC machining centres, as well as drill jigs, turning fixtures, assembly fixtures and fixtures for special purpose machines.

What do you need to design a fixture?

Your component drawing, the operations to be performed, the machine it will be used on and the production volume. A sample component also helps.

Next step: share your desired production rate, relevant part drawings and any machine concept. Our application engineers will review them and show how they can be achieved, without obligation.
