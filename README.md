# Buckeye Manufacturing & Automation Solutions

## About Buckeye Machines
Buckeye Machines specializes in the design, engineering, and manufacturing of custom Special Purpose Machines (SPMs), CNC Production Machines, Assembly Automation Systems, Endurance Testing Rigs, Press Shop Automation, and High-Precision Fixtures.

---

## Key Offerings & Capabilities

### 1. Special Purpose Machines (SPMs)
Custom-built, high-productivity machinery optimized for complex metal cutting, drilling, reaming, milling, and multi-axis machining operations.
* **Rotary Index Machines:** Multi-station rotary index systems for high-throughput component processing (e.g., compressor blocks, engine components).
* **Multi-Spindle Drilling & Reaming:** Heavy-duty multi-spindle heads for multi-hole processing in a single stroke.
* **Key Bitting & Dimpling Machines:** Precision automated machinery for lock component manufacturing.

### 2. CNC Production Machines
High-efficiency, custom CNC-integrated platforms engineered for dedicated, high-volume component manufacturing.
* **Teeth Rolling Machines:** Precision cold-forming systems.
* **Multi-Station CNC Systems:** Combining turning, milling, and indexing capabilities into single-station setups.

### 3. Assembly Machines & Automation
Automated assembly lines designed to streamline complex multi-component assembly operations with inline quality verification.
* **Flashlight Assembly Lines:** Fully automated insertion, feeding, and testing sequences.
* **Dry Cell Battery Lines:** High-speed continuous assembly systems.

### 4. Endurance & Functional Testing Rig
Automated test benches to measure durability, fatigue, and functional performance of mechanical components.
* **Pin Cylinder & Tumbler Testing Rigs**
* **Mortice Lock & Door Handle Test Benches**
* **Drawer Slide Fatigue & Endurance Testers**

### 5. Press Shop Automation & Tooling
Custom progressive die feeders, transfer mechanisms, and high-speed stamping automation solutions.

### 6. Precision Workholding & Fixtures
Hydraulic, pneumatic, and manual workholding fixtures built for high-precision CNC machining centers.
* Hydraulic valve body machining fixtures.
* Sewing machine housing power clamping fixtures.
* Tractor engine part milling & center housing reaming fixtures.
