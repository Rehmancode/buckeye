FAQ

Questions engineers ask us.

What do I need to send for a quote?

Send your desired production rate, relevant part drawings and any machine concept or preference you have. Our application engineers review your requirements and show how they can be achieved, without obligation.

Which industries do you serve?

We build for automotive, tractor, appliance, fan, battery, lock and sewing machine manufacturers, among others. Customers include Escorts, Bajaj Auto, Mahindra, Singer and Godrej.

Do you supply machines outside India?

Yes. We have built assembly machines for customers in Bangladesh and Taiwan, and supplied a machine to Aluthea Group in Portugal.

What can your multi-spindle drilling and tapping machines do?

They drill and tap holes of any size, quantity or pattern in a single cycle, and can also ream, counterbore, countersink, spot face, chamfer and deep-hole drill.

Which standards do your endurance testing machines follow?

They are built for hardware products to BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) guidelines by a dedicated team set up in 2024.

How many machines can you build in a year?

Our capacity is about 36 special purpose machines of average size per year. We work with four to eight customers at a time so each gets our full focus.
