Where it fits

Endurance testing repeats a product’s working motion many times to show how long it lasts. For hardware makers it turns a durability claim into a measured result. Our machines are built for BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) guidelines.

Our endurance testing division was set up in 2024 in response to customer requirements and growing demand from the hardware industry. A dedicated team of engineers, designers and technicians builds the machines, with experience in controls, measurement and inspection techniques.

How a project runs

  1. Study. We review your product and the standard or test requirement it must meet.
  2. Design. The test motion, loading and counting are designed around the product.
  3. Build. The machine is built by our dedicated endurance testing team.
  4. Trials. The machine is run with your product to confirm the test works as intended.
  5. Delivery and support. We deliver the machine and support it in use.

Frequently asked questions

Which products can your machines test?

We have built endurance testing machines for pin cylinder tumblers, for the door handles of mortice locks and for telescopic ball bearing drawer slides.

Which standards do the machines follow?

They are built for BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) guidelines, and our catalog also refers to international standards. Tell us the standard your product must meet and we will design to it.

Can you build a machine for a product not on your list?

Send the product drawing and the test requirement. Our team designs endurance testing machines to suit the hardware product and the standard.

Next step: share your desired production rate, relevant part drawings and any machine concept. Our application engineers will review them and show how they can be achieved, without obligation.
