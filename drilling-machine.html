Multi-Spindle Drilling and Tapping Machines

Holes: any size, any quantity, any pattern.

Buckeye fixed-centre multi-spindle heads are designed to your specific requirements. Our engineers evaluate clearances, input speeds, horsepower and the mounting characteristics of your equipment. Spindle location, tool sizes and workpiece material are considered before the gear train is laid out, and standard spindle designs are used wherever possible for rigidity and maintenance-free service.

The layout is sent to you for approval before detailing, and a complete set of assembly and detail drawings with parts lists is made available.

Buckeye multi-spindle drilling and tapping machines are built to your exact requirement for holes in any quantity, size or pattern. They perform drilling, tapping and deep-hole drilling, plus counterboring, spot facing and chamfering. Priced below comparable automatic-cycle machines with hydraulic table feed or CNC-controlled cycle.

A Buckeye machine is designed to meet your cost per part, parts per hour, quality and predicted payout requirements year after year. The machine pays for itself.

Where it fits

A multi-spindle head drills or taps every hole in a pattern at the same time, so a component with many holes is machined in one stroke instead of one hole at a time. That cuts machining time, labour cost and handling together.

Buckeye makes both fixed-centre drill heads and complete fully automatic multi-spindle drilling and tapping machines. Our first machine, built in 1978, was a three-way multi-spindle drilling machine for a tractor cylinder head.

How a project runs

  1. Study. We evaluate clearances, input speeds, horsepower, spindle locations, tool sizes and workpiece material.
  2. Layout approval. The layout is sent to you for approval before detailing.
  3. Detailing. A full set of assembly and detail drawings with parts lists is prepared.
  4. Build and inspection. Cover plates and gearboxes are checked for hole spacing and bore size before assembly.
  5. Delivery. The head or machine is delivered to meet your cost per part, parts per hour and quality targets.

Frequently asked questions

What hole sizes and patterns can you handle?

Any size, quantity or pattern. Heads are designed to your specific hole pattern after we review clearances, speeds, horsepower and mounting characteristics.

How accurate is spindle positioning?

Spindle run-out is held to 0.02 mm and spindle spacing to ±0.02 mm, both non-accumulative. Closer tolerances are available on request on precision heads.

Do your machines also tap?

Yes. Machines are available for multi-spindle drilling, tapping, or drilling and tapping together, plus reaming, counterboring, countersinking, spot facing, chamfering and deep-hole drilling.
